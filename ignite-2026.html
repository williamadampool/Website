Microsoft Ignite 2026: the best sessions are from the trenches

Another Microsoft Ignite is in the books, and as always, it was a good and interesting week. It kicked off with flight madness into San Francisco, which was getting record amounts of rain. Not the smoothest start, but we made it.

What made the week was the sessions put on by the people behind Windows. Not the C-suite, but the folks in the trenches who actually do the work every day. Seeing their excitement, and hearing the same worries and frustrations I deal with, was the best part.

When people who run this stuff in production get on stage and speak honestly, you learn more in 45 minutes than in any keynote. I'm leaving hopeful and excited for what's coming. Until next year, Microsoft.
