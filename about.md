I'm a **systems administrator** working in a hybrid environment — **Nutanix** hyperconverged infrastructure on-prem, a deep **Microsoft** stack, and an ever-growing footprint in **Azure**. My job is to keep things running, keep them secure, and leave them a little better documented than I found them.

Right now I'm learning **C#** so I can go beyond scripts and build real tools: small utilities, API integrations, and automation that other admins can actually use.

## How I work

- **Understand first.** I'd rather know *why* something broke than just make the alert go away.
- **Automate the boring parts.** If it's done twice, it gets scripted. If it's done three times, it gets documented.
- **Hack life, not prod.** Curiosity is great. Change control is also great.

## Outside of work

I'm a technology nerd with a soft spot for the physical world: weather systems, geology, and anything with an engine I can take apart. Most weekends you'll find me outside — on a trail, around a fire, or under a car.

## This site

This is where I write down what I learn — fixes, homelab experiments, C# progress, and the occasional storm-chasing tangent. If one of these posts saves you an hour of troubleshooting, it did its job.
