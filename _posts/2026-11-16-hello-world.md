Every sysadmin has a graveyard of half-finished OneNote pages, sticky notes, and "I'll remember this" fixes that they absolutely did not remember. This blog is my attempt to fix that — in public.

<!--more-->

## What you'll find here

- **Nutanix, Windows Server, and Azure** — fixes, how-tos, and the "why" behind them.
- **Learning C#** — my progress going from PowerShell scripts to real compiled tools.
- **Homelab experiments** — breaking things on purpose so I don't break them at work.
- **The outside world** — weather, geology, engines, and trails. Systems thinking applies everywhere.

## Why write it down?

Explaining something is the fastest way to find out whether you actually understand it. If I can't write a clear post about it, I don't know it yet.

> Documentation is a love letter to your future self. — every sysadmin, eventually

See you in the logs.
