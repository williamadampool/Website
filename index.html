About

What I do and how I think about it.

I lead cloud and datacenter operations for the City of Alpharetta, Georgia, keeping the systems that city services and public safety depend on available, secure, and recoverable.

My work spans Nutanix and virtualization, Microsoft Azure and Entra ID, PostgreSQL databases, disaster recovery, and backup. I've led cloud migrations, hybrid network buildouts, and licensing transitions, and I own the RFPs, purchasing, and contract renewals for my teams.

I like work where the stakes are real: building recovery plans that hold up, automating admin work with PowerShell and C#, and explaining technical trade-offs so leaders can make good decisions. Outside of work, I'm a self-described "hacker of life," curious about how things work, from servers to engines to the weather.
